# Cuss for Canvas — 90-second pitch

## Opening

College students do not have an information problem. They have a fragmentation problem. Their deadlines are in one Canvas view, readings in another, announcements in a third, and the professor they need to email is somewhere else entirely.

## Product

Cuss is the AI study assistant that connects to Canvas and turns that scattered information into action. Ask, “What is due this week?” and it checks the real planner. Ask for help with a topic and it reads the course material first. Ask for flashcards and it builds them from the module you are actually studying.

It can also draft a respectful email to the right instructor, create a practice quiz, remember how you learn, and give you a daily briefing before the school day starts.

## Demo sequence

1. Scan the QR code and choose **Try the demo**.
2. Ask: “What is due this week? Anything missing?”
3. Ask: “Explain osmosis from my biology course.”
4. Ask: “Make me five flashcards from Week 8.”
5. Ask: “Draft an email to Dr. Chen asking about the lab.”
6. Show that the email is a draft and requires the student to press Send.

## Optional professor answer

If judges ask how Cuss could support instructors, switch to the professor demo:

> "The same role-aware product also has Cuss for Professors: it shows class health, surfaces submitted work waiting for grading, and drafts announcements or kind student nudges. Those actions stay drafts until the professor approves them."

## Close

Cuss is not another generic chatbot. It is a student-controlled layer on top of the LMS students already use. We built the demo around privacy, grounding, and safe actions—and the next step is making that experience native on iOS and Android for every campus.
