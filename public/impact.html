What we measure, and why

  • Missing assignments over time: each student's count of missing work, recorded daily from Canvas. Goal: it goes down after a student starts using Cuss.
  • Reminders delivered before a deadline, and whether the work then gets submitted on time.
  • Emails and replies to professors that students actually sent, a proxy for asking for help instead of staying stuck.
  • Practice quizzes and scores from real course material, a proxy for studying earlier and with feedback.

Pilot plan at SF State

  • Start with 20–30 SF State students, including students with ADHD and other disabilities, with a short survey before and after 4 weeks: stress about deadlines, and confidence emailing professors.
  • Compare each student's missing-assignment count and on-time submission rate in the 4 weeks before and after joining.
  • Share results with students and the university. Students can delete their data at any time.
