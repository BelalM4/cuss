Privacy

Plain-English summary of what Cuss does with your information.

What Cuss can see

When you sign in with your school's Canvas, Cuss can read what you can already see in Canvas: your courses, assignments and due dates, grades, course pages and files, announcements, and your Canvas Inbox. Cuss never sees your password; you sign in on your school's own login page.

What Cuss stores

  • Your Canvas sign-in token, so Cuss can act on your behalf.
  • Your chats, saved notes ("what Cuss remembers"), email drafts and settings.

This information is only used to run Cuss for you and is never shared with other students or sold.

AI processing

To answer you, the course content and messages you ask about are sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models by default.

Sending messages

Cuss only sends a Canvas message or reply after you press Send, unless you turn on "Send emails without asking me" in Settings.

Your choices

You can log out at any time. Settings → Delete my data & disconnect erases everything Cuss stored about you and revokes its access to your Canvas account.

Academic integrity

Cuss is designed to help you learn and stay organized. It won't complete graded work for you or answer live quizzes and exams.
